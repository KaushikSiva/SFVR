FIELD NOTES / 01

OWN THE SKY.

Shoot drones before they breach the park. Survive 60 seconds with at least one shield.

Aim & fire
Move your mouse and click. Hold to fire repeatedly. On touchscreens, tap a drone.
Keep it going
12 shots per magazine. Press R or tap your ammo to reload; an empty magazine reloads automatically.
Build a combo
Clear drones within 3 seconds of each other for up to 5× points. Gold drones are worth extra; purple drones take two hits.
In your headset
Open this page in Chrome on Galaxy XR, then select Enter Galaxy XR. Point and pinch, or aim a controller and pull its trigger. Select once to start or replay. Your physical head movement controls the view.

WebXR needs a secure URL or localhost through ADB reverse. Point and pinch to fire. Keep your play area clear while using the headset.
