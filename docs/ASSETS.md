# Asset credits

SFVR reuses authored geometry from the user's CORTEX environment and adds its
own game, park details, blasters and drones. The location is an approximation;
the sky and materials are illustrative, not a capture of Alamo Square.

| Included asset | Source and license |
| --- | --- |
| Victorian paint and roof textures | [Blue Painted Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/blue_painted_planks) and [Roof Slates 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/roof_slates_03), Poly Haven, CC0 |
| Asphalt textures | [Asphalt 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02), Poly Haven, CC0 |
| Grass textures | [Aerial Grass Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock), Poly Haven, CC0 |
| Path textures | [Concrete Floor Worn 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001), Poly Haven, CC0 |
| HDR sky and lighting | [Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy), Greg Zaal / Poly Haven, CC0 |
| Park tree | Authored bent-leaf geometry using derived [Island Tree 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02) materials, Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers / Poly Haven, CC0. Full derivation notes in `park-tree.attribution.json` |
| Parked hatchback | Original authored geometry/materials, CC0. Illustrative placements; original reference notes in `parked-hatchback.SOURCE.json` |
| Neighborhood footprints and elevations | City and County of San Francisco Open Data Program, Enterprise GIS Program, Department of Environment and Department of Technology. [PDDL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/pddl/1-0/). Query, units and limitations in `neighborhood/SOURCE.json` |

The buildings are full-volume authored geometry. Materials and simplified
background façades are not surveyed replicas.

Demo narration uses ElevenLabs' stock Brian voice, generated for this project.
The speech is generated audio; it is not the recorded voice of a team member.
Screenshots and gameplay footage are captures of the running application.
