This is S F V R, a shooting game built for Samsung Galaxy XR.
Step into San Francisco's Alamo Square, surrounded by the Painted Ladies, a detailed park, and incoming drones.
In the headset, your hands become the controls. Point to aim, pinch to fire, and hold your pinch for a burst. No handheld controller is required.
You have sixty seconds and three shields. Clear targets before they break through. Gold drones reward precision, armored drones take two hits, and quick eliminations build your combo.
Textured surfaces, directional shadows, and detailed three-dimensional equipment give the arena depth as you move your head.
The game runs locally in Chrome on Galaxy XR, using its Qualcomm Snapdragon hardware. This version uses WebXR rendering, with no AI inference claimed.
Connect the headset, open the game, and select Enter Galaxy XR.
S F V R. Defend the skyline with your hands.
